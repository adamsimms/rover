# Montreal fabrication access

Electronics subassembly (camera, Pi, telecom) can be prepped in Montreal and brought to Newfoundland once the frame is ready. Montreal has a dense network of library makerspaces and community fab labs with 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC, and electronics access — enough to cover that off-critical-path work without waiting for a fabrication-focused residency.

## Community fab labs (membership)

**ÉchoFab** (Communautique, Montreal Innovation District): multidisciplinary digital-fabrication workshop — 3D printing, CNC, laser, circuit production, milling. Membership-oriented; Communautique's flagship citizen digital-fabrication studio.

**Fab Lab du PEC** (Pavillon d'éducation communautaire, Hochelaga-Maisonneuve): full digital-fab stack, low-cost membership (~$30), reserved machine hours, free member coaching.

**L'Espace Maker** (2875 Hochelaga, Centre-Sud): co-op makerspace with wood, metal, 3D printing, CNC, laser, and precision milling; member studio rental for longer projects.

## Free library-network labs (Ville de Montréal card)

**Pierrefonds Makerspace** (Pierrefonds Library): Ultimaker printers, Trotec Speedy 100 laser, electronics/robotics kits; free with Montreal library membership.

**Fabricathèque du Sud-Ouest** (Bibliothèque St-Henri): fab-lab-inspired with CNC-adjacent machines and audiovisual gear; accessed through activity program.

## South Shore

**Fab Lab Brossard** (7855 Avenue San Francisco, inside Brossard public library): laser cutter, desktop CNC, filament and resin 3D printers, 3D scanner; free for Brossard residents (consumables billed separately). Useful if South Shore access is easier.

## Institutional (restricted access)

**PolyFab Normand Brais** (Polytechnique Montréal): strong equipment community (3D printing, CNC, laser, circuit production); oriented to the Polytechnique campus, not open walk-in.

**LCC Fab Lab** (Lower Canada College): Fab Charter–committed; public access mainly by appointment or workshops — useful if affiliation opens a door.
